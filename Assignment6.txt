Assignment 6 — RELAY: a shared kinetic playground

Scene: relay_arena

RELAY adapts bouncing.js into a cooperative game for two or more players.
Touch an orb to turn it amber; a second distinct player makes it mint green.
Send charged orbs through the mint gate. Score eight goals within 90 seconds.
Two hands from the same player do not count as two participants.

Spatial widgets (using G2.addWidget, following widgets.js):
- Launch/restart button at the front-left launch dock.
- Gravity slider above the playing space.
- Speed slider next to the arena's right side.
- Shared scoreboard behind the goal.

Multiplayer:
The lowest active client ID runs fixed-timestep physics and sends snapshots
through the course server.send/server.sync messaging API. Players share hand
positions, widget commands, ball contacts, scores, timer and settings. If the
host leaves, remaining participants elect a replacement. Play pauses with
fewer than two active players.

Run with Node 18:
  node server/main.js 2026 2026 http
Open http://localhost:2026 and select relay_arena in two browser windows.
Enable desktop paddle in each window and move it with the X/Y/Z sliders.
In VR, touch orbs with your controllers and use the trigger/ray for widgets.
Headsets require the course's trusted HTTPS setup and a shared spatial origin.
The included run-hw6.command uses the author's installed Node 18 runtime;
other computers should use the command above with their own Node 18 install.
